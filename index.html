What we build

Purpose-built container conversions

Every conversion starts as a standard 3m, 6m, 12m or 12m High Cube container and comes out fitted for exactly what your site needs.

Standard 6m GP storage containers

  • B & C grade storage containers for sale
  • Ideal for all your storage requirements
  • Delivered with a crane truck and placed in your required position
  • Containers are ex depot, unpainted
  • Viewing of the containers is welcome

Site offices standard

Includes a wind & water tight C-grade container, fitted out as follows:

  • 2 x 900mm x 900mm aluminium windows with burglar bars
  • 1 x steel panel door with a lockset
  • Electrical DB, 4 x double plugs, 2 x 4ft LED lights, light switch (C.O.C. on request)
  • Painted interior & exterior with primer and enamel top coat
  • Flooring painted with a wood sealant treatment

Pricing from R63 000 ex VAT & delivery — delivery quoted separately based on destination.

Site offices executive

Includes a wind & water tight B-grade container, fitted out as follows:

  • 2 x 900mm x 900mm aluminium windows with burglar bars
  • 1 x steel panel door with a lockset
  • Interior insulated with 40mm ISO panels, with either a grooved or smooth finish
  • Electrical DB, 4 x double plugs, 2 x 4ft LED lights, light switch (C.O.C. on request)
  • A 12000BTU split unit aircon fitted with an outdoor isolator for safety
  • Painted on the exterior with primer and enamel top coat
  • Flooring rubberised with a polyurethane spray of 3mm thickness for durability and longevity

Pricing from R99 000 ex VAT & delivery — delivery quoted separately based on destination.

Store rooms
