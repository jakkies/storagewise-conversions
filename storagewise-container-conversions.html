What we build

One container. Any purpose.

Every conversion starts as a standard 3m, 6m, 12m or 12m High Cube container and comes out fitted for exactly what your site needs.

Site Offices

A dry, lockable office for the site manager — ready before the first slab is poured. Wired, insulated and windowed on request.

Ablution Blocks

Toilets, basins and showers built into a container shell, plumbed and sealed for a busy construction or event site.

Guard Houses

A secure, weatherproof post at your gate with windows positioned for a full view of the entrance and access road.

Accommodation Units

Insulated, wired living space for site or farm staff — from a single room to a full multi-container unit.

Workshops & Store Rooms

Reinforced shelving, benches and walkways between linked containers, built around the tools and stock you run.

Shops & Kiosks

Spaza shops and kiosk conversions with a serving hatch, roller shutter and space for signage and branding.

Before you ask

Common questions

How long does a conversion take?
It depends on the size and finish. Most single-container conversions are quoted within days and built within a few weeks — we'll confirm a firm date when we quote your job.
Do you use new or used containers?
Both. Tell us your budget and intended use and we'll recommend new, one-trip, or refurbished (Grade B/C) stock.
Do you deliver outside Pretoria and Centurion?
Yes — we deliver and place containers nationwide, including remote mining and construction sites.
Is the electrical work certified?
Yes. Every electrical installation is supplied with a Certificate of Compliance (C.O.C.).
Can you meet our company's procurement requirements?
We're BEE certified and can supply the relevant certificates and documentation for your procurement process.
